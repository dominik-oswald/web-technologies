Dominik Oswald — @hearthead.ozzy

I'm a Zürich-based interaction design student and work multidisciplinary. Art, graphic design, illustration, photography, and videography are all under my umbrella of practice.

My work sits between research and personal practice — I treat each project as an entry in an ongoing archive. I'm drawn to dense type, archival aesthetics, raw paper, ink that bleeds slightly, interfaces that feel like reading rooms, and small details that betray a human hand.

Currently studying interaction design while taking freelance commissions in editorial design, illustration and product UI. Available for collaborations, commissions and slow conversations.
